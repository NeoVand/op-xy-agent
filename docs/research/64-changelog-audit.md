# 64 — Changelog audit: every OS change checked against the replica

> Research date 2026-10-02. Reference firmware OS 1.1.33 (the owner's unit, `docs/DECISIONS.md` D3).
> This note answers: is there firmware newer than 1.1.33, which of the 300 changelog items from OS
> 1.0.9 to 1.1.33 change how the OP-XY behaves, does our replica (simulator, sound engine, app) do
> what each of them says, does our manual say it, and where would following TE's guide (v1.1.15) make
> the replica wrong. Sources: the changelog scrape in `knowledge/official/changelog.{md,json}`
> (fetched 2026-09-26), TE's downloads page, `releases.json`, the guide re-fetched on 2026-10-02, and
> community threads (§1). Every changelog line is paraphrased here (D2); third-party posts are
> paraphrased too. Code paths are relative to `src/lib/` unless they start with `knowledge/` or
> `docs/`; line numbers are those of commit `87e67cb`. Nothing was sent to the device for this note;
> raw pages are in the git-ignored `research/web/changelog-audit/`.

## TL;DR

- **No firmware newer than 1.1.33.** The downloads page's content (its decoded CMS tree) hashes
  the same as our 2026-09-26 scrape, TE's `releases.json` still names 1.1.33, no file exists at the
  predictable download addresses for 1.1.34 to 1.1.50, 1.2.0 or 1.2.1, a firmware tracker last
  checked on 2026-10-01 lists 1.1.33, and no beta appears in the community threads. The online
  guide is still v1.1.15 and all 25 of its pages are unchanged.
- **300 items reviewed** (22 releases): 112 are not relevant to a replica (crashes, stability,
  speed, glitches, TRS/BLE/MTP plumbing); **188 are relevant**: 108 implemented, 19 partial,
  3 in conflict with the changelog, 28 missing, 17 unclear, 13 outside what a browser can model.
  The manual covers 117 of them, 7 partly; 64 are not in it (mostly small fixes).
- **The biggest gaps** are not in the newest releases but in older behaviour the replica never
  sounds: linked tracks, project transpose, the send effects' settings, preset modulation routing
  and drum pitch modulation. Among the post-guide changes: a latched arpeggio should stop when the
  pattern changes (1.1.21), the voice-steal indicator was reworked (1.1.25), the sample library
  marks sub-folders with "/" (1.1.17, the guide says square brackets), templates can be made the
  default (1.1.17).
- **Three conflicts** need the device: the value LFO's slowest free speed (our CC measurement says
  it stands still, 1.1.15 says it moves), the sub-folder mark, and the voice-steal indicator.
- **54 device checks** (D1–D54, §6) settle what the changelog states only briefly; the ranked gaps
  in §4 say which matter most.

## 2. How the audit was done

Each of the 300 lines got a kind (new **feature**, **change** of behaviour, or **fix**) and a
relevance: **R**, behaviour a faithful replica or our manual must model; **L**, a small fix that only
confirms the intended behaviour; **N**, nothing to model (crash, stability, speed, audio glitch,
unspecified "ui tweaks", TRS/BLE/MTP robustness). For the 188 R and L items we read the code path
that would produce the behaviour (not keyword hits) in `sim/`, `sound/`, `replica/`, `app/` and, where
relevant, `core/`, preferred a pinned test (`sim/conformance/*.cases.ts`, the specs) as evidence, and
looked the fact up in `knowledge/manual/units/` (facts citing `downloads/op-xy#<version>`, plus the
guide-sourced ones). Replica statuses: **implemented** (does what the item says, as far as code and
tests show); **partial** (some of it; the gap is named); **conflict** (does something the changelog
contradicts); **missing**; **unclear** (the item is too vague, or the code's behaviour can't be
settled without the device); **n/a** (hardware the browser lacks: BLE, TRS, gyroscope, MIDI out).

## 4. The gaps that matter most (ranked)

Ranked by how often a user would hit them and how far the replica is off. Each says what to settle on
the unit and how; everything runs on a scratch project that is never saved unless marked.

1. **Linked tracks make no sound.** Guide 6.2, 1.0.15 #3 (bend reaches the linked tracks), #4
   (arpeggio notes stay on the primary), 1.1.15 #14 (new octave-offset logic). The replica stores
   links and dims their keys (`sim/opxy-sim.svelte.ts:459-480`, `sim/frames.ts:304-313`) but neither
   the scheduler nor the live path plays a linked track. **Verify (D16):** hold T3, press T4; play
   C4 on T3 and record USB audio (T3 alone, then linked); bend while holding; switch T3's arpeggio
   on; step-enter a note on T3; set T4's keyboard octave +1 before linking and note which octave T4
   plays.
2. **Project transpose is never heard.** 1.0.9 #10 (drums excluded), 1.1.21 #4 (simplified),
   1.1.25 #2 (multisamples follow better); the guide says drums follow. The setting is stored and
   exported (`sim/areas/system/settings.ts:107-113`, `sim/xy.ts`) but nothing in `sound/` reads it.
   **Verify (D54, D4):** read the transpose range (±12 here; research 10 asks whether the byte is
   still ±24); +5 with drums on T1 and a synth on T3: do the drums change? With the brain on auto and
   T3 routed: +2: does the brain's key move with it, or do chords re-harmonise? T8's multisampler at
   +5: does the next zone take over?
3. **The send effects ignore their pages, and FX tracks ignore patterns.** 1.0.25 #7 (FX-track
   settings follow the pattern), 1.0.50 #22 (delay size shown as note values: drawn only).
   `sound/fx.ts` builds one fixed dotted-eighth ping-pong delay and one hall reverb; chorus,
   distortion, lofi and phaser return silence; T7/T8's M1 values, drawn from the captures, never reach
   the sound; the simulator keeps one FX setting per project (`sim/areas/auxiliary/state.ts`). This
   is broader than the changelog but every user touches it. **Verify (D46):** two T7 patterns with
   different delay settings (or a lock), switch while playing, watch M1 and listen. To build the
   effects, record each type at a few settings (research 60 measured none).
4. **Preset settings and drum modulation are silent.** 1.1.0 #15 (velocity → drum filter), #14
   (LFO → drum pitch), 1.1.25 #10 (user tunings). Only the preset's velocity sensitivity reaches the
   sound (`sound/engine.ts:444`); the mod routing of mod wheel, aftertouch, bend and velocity, the
   tunings, high-pass, width and portamento type are stored but unheard, and a drum voice offers the
   LFO no pitch target (`sound/synths.ts:679-711`). **Verify:** drum track, shift + instrument, route
   velocity to the filter cutoff at 99 with the filter low; record soft and hard hits. Value LFO on
   the drum page's tune: record the wobble. Pick a user tuning slot and record a scale.
5. **A latched arpeggio survives a pattern switch.** 1.1.21 #3. Nothing clears the arpeggio's kept
   notes when the pattern changes, only a track change or stop (`sim/areas/sequencer/sim.ts:214-220`
   says so). **Verify (D5):** arpeggio with hold on, latch a chord while playing, move to the next
   pattern in arrange (E4), then by a scene change: does it stop at once?
6. **Track scale values.** 1.0.15 #8 (1/5 and 1/7 exist), 1.1.25 #1 (x5 and x7 exist). The
   replica offers 1–8, 16 and 1/2 on the ten black keys (`sim/sequencer.ts:90`; only key 4 is TE's,
   the rest our reading) and has no 1/5 or 1/7; the `.xy` byte is known for four values. **Verify
   (D49, D3):** hold bar, press each black key, photograph "track scale"; optionally copy the scratch
   project off over MTP after each (read-only for the unit) to decode the byte.
7. **Usage indicators follow the guide, not 1.1.25.** 1.1.15 #7, 1.1.25 #7, 1.0.50 #20, 1.1.32 #3.
   The replica estimates voices, CPU and memory from the state (`sim/areas/system/usage.ts`, whose
   header still says the replica has no audio engine) and shows the voice icon from 17 voices; 1.1.25
   shows every audible steal, and the CPU icon should follow dropped notes. The sound engine's own
   allocator (`sound/allocator.ts`) could drive it. **Verify (D2):** 24+ voices (long chords on three
   tracks), then a track limited to two voices (project settings) playing triads: when does each icon
   show, does it blink?
8. **Value LFO at its slowest free speed (conflict).** 1.1.15 #19 says the slowest manual speed no
   longer stops; research 60 §4 measured the LFO standing still at CC 64, and `sound/mapping.ts:389`

   free position and record two minutes with full amount on the cutoff.
9. **Long notes re-attack; same pitches cut each other.** 1.0.40 #2 (a drone that is still sounding
   is not retriggered), 1.0.38 #2 (a note can sound over the same note). The scheduler re-emits every

   with full sustain; two overlapping same-pitch notes.

    notes duck even from a silenced source. The replica never schedules a muted or midi-engine

    T4 with source T3; mute T3 (mix): does T4 still pump? Source 11 (external MIDI) with a pattern.
11. **Templates cannot be made the default.** 1.1.17 #2; the replica's M3 duplicates in every
    folder (`sim/areas/system/sim.ts:338-374`). **Verify (D9, changes a setting: announce):** in the

12. **The midi engine on 1.1.33.** 1.0.15 #6, 1.0.45 #12–#13, 1.0.50 #16–#18, 1.1.15 #28 and
    QUESTIONS #10: the owner's preset browser listed no midi engine, so how 1.1.33 puts a track on

13. **The sample library on 1.1.33.** 1.1.17 #13 (sub-folder mark "/", conflict), 1.1.15 #5 (a
    group of the samples user presets use), 1.0.9 #12 (sfx and vox folders), 1.1.15 #6 and 1.0.32 #7
    (previews: the replica sets the flag but never plays it). **Verify (D6, D17, D52):** photograph the

    (1.0.38 #14, D35), the charging option hidden off power (1.1.3 #5, D19).

## 8. Noticed along the way

- `sim/areas/system/presets.ts:541` scrambles the filter's envelope amount over −99…99, while the
  field is 0–99 (`sim/params.ts:203-207`, presets store it unsigned).
- `sim/areas/arrange/model.ts` credits "only add a pattern when the current one is not empty" to OS
  1.0.29; it is a 1.0.25 change.
- `sim/areas/system/usage.ts` says the replica has no audio engine; it has one, with a 24-voice
  allocator the indicators could read.
- The tape track, the aux tracks' filter and LFO pages (`sim/areas/auxiliary/state.ts` AuxPages),
  the sample library's previews and the record page's take playback are state only: nothing in
  `sound/` plays or applies them.
- The arpeggio never runs on a midi-engine track in the replica (`sound/scheduler.ts` arpeggioInput),
  so 1.1.32 #8 cannot even arise there.
- `releases.json` pairs 1.1.33 with 1.1.32's notes (§1).
