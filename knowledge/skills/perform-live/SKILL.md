# Playing it live

Live, nothing stops: every move happens while the loop plays, so teach the moves that work on the
fly, one at a time, and let the user try each while it runs.

## The moves

- **Bring tracks in and out:** `instrument + T1` mutes or unmutes track 1 at once (in mix mode,
  `shift + T1`); a mute stops new notes while what already sounds rings out, and a scene remembers
  the mutes it was saved with.
- **Switch sections on the beat:** queue the next scene (`shift`, tap `play`, then its black key)
  instead of switching mid-bar ([arrange.scene-queue]); a scene picked directly takes over at once and
  repeats ([howto.loop-one-scene]).
- **Punch-in effects:** in auxiliary mode `T2` is the punch-in FX track; each keyboard key is an effect
  for as long as it is held, and several stack ([howto.first-punch-in]). They record and sequence
  like any other track.
- **Tempo and groove:** the tempo page changes both while it plays; tap tempo follows a drummer.
- **Play over it:** the keys play the selected track in time; the players (arpeggio, hold, maestro)
  turn held notes into patterns ([players.overview]).
- **Filter moves:** a slow turn of a track's cutoff is the oldest trick there is, and it works on
  every track.

## Teaching it

Keep the replica playing while you show a move. Light the keys for the user to press (the teaching
skill's walkthrough), one move per turn, and let them repeat it until it feels natural. A short
practice idea helps: "bring the drums in on the one", "queue the chorus and drop it on the next bar".
For a performance they want to repeat, suggest recording the moves into patterns so the song plays
them by itself.
