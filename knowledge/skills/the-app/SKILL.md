# The app around the replica

- **The manual** (the header's "manual") is our own OP-XY manual, one page per unit, with every step
  drawn as the device's keys; clicking a combination plays it on a replica on the page. A citation
  in your answer opens its page, so citing is also how you hand the user a deeper read.
- **The computer keyboard plays the replica's 24 keys**: the Z row plays the lower twelve, with the
  black keys on the row above; the Q row plays the upper twelve, with the black keys on the digits.
  `-` and `=` are `[-]` and `[+]`, Space is play or stop, and shift + 1–9 or 0 are the numbered black
  keys. The "keys" switch under the replica turns this off.
- **The project card** (the "project" key under the replica): open or download a `.xy` project (how
  what was made here is saved to the computer; the OP-XY's own projects copy off it over MTP,
  `com → M4`, as the manual's back-up recipe gives), load the one the OP-XY has open, save to it,
  start a new project (the device's own "new project"), and undo the last load. Its download row saves the song as audio ("song .wav": every scene of the song
  rendered through the replica's own sound, a few seconds' work) or as MIDI ("song .mid", one track
  per OP-XY track); ⌘K's "download the song as midi" does the second. That is how to get a WAV of
  what was made here; the OP-XY itself has no bounce, so its own audio is recorded over USB.
- **Undo**: every change you make to the replica can be undone from the app.
- **Sound**: browsers start audio only after a click on the page; the sound key under the replica
  switches the replica's sound on and off. The replica's record page arms and runs as the unit's
  does, but takes no audio from the computer's mic or inputs: sampling a voice happens on the OP-XY
  (or a sample made in the preset maker is loaded onto a key).
- **Connecting**: the connect key under the replica links the user's OP-XY over USB (Web MIDI, in
  Chrome). With track MIDI channels set on the unit, what they play lights the replica too.
