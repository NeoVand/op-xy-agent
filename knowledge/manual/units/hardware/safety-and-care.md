  - id: connect
    text: Switch every device off before cabling them together; when you have to plug in while running, plug the lead into the OP-XY before the other device.

  - id: phantom
    text: Never run a cable from the OP-XY's jacks into a microphone input with phantom power switched on; it can damage the unit.
    source: https://teenage.engineering/guides/op-xy
  - id: charging
    text: Charge only from 5 V USB power, such as a computer's USB port or a USB charger.
    source: https://teenage.engineering/guides/op-xy
  - id: temperature
    text: Use the unit between 10 and 35 °C (50–95 °F) and store it between 0 and 35 °C (32–86 °F).
    source: https://teenage.engineering/guides/op-xy
  - id: cleaning
    text: Wipe the shell with a slightly damp cloth and let it dry before use.
    source: https://teenage.engineering/guides/op-xy
  - id: battery
    text: Do not charge or use a unit whose battery looks damaged; replacement batteries must come from TE and be fitted by qualified staff, and heat, fire or crushing can make a battery explode or leak.
    source: https://teenage.engineering/guides/op-xy
  - id: hearing
    text: Avoid long listening sessions at high volume to protect your hearing.
    source: https://teenage.engineering/guides/op-xy
  - id: warranty
    text: The unit is factory tested and has a 12-month warranty from the purchase date; it does not cover misuse (drops, crushing, wrong voltages on the connectors, faulty modifications) or shipping costs.

The two that catch people out: phantom power on a mixer or interface channel the OP-XY is plugged
into, and charging from anything but plain 5 V USB.
